The Problem
Your app shows earnings.
It doesn't show what you actually kept.
Gas, deductible expenses, and estimated taxes aren't calculated in the different platform apps. GigWork Tracker fills that gap and tracks it for you.

How It Works
The gas math, explained
This is the same method careful drivers already use with a notebook — GigWork Tracker just automates it.
1

Log two fill-ups

Odometer, gallons, and price per gallon each time you fill up.

2

App calculates your MPG

Miles between fill-ups ÷ gallons used = your real-world MPG for that stretch.

3

Every shift gets priced

Your shift's miles ÷ MPG × price per gallon = exactly what that shift cost you in gas.

Get Started
Add it to your Home Screen
No App Store, no download — it installs like an app straight from your browser in under a minute.
On iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari or Chrome — both work the same way on iPhone.
  2. Tap "Open the App" above, then tap the Share button — a square with an arrow pointing up. In Safari it's at the bottom of the screen; in Chrome it's near the top.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen," then tap Add.
On Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Chrome may show an "Install app" banner or icon automatically — if so, tap that. If not, tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Look for "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" — the exact wording can vary by device — then confirm.
Steps can look slightly different depending on your phone and browser version — if you don't see an exact match, look for anything mentioning "Home Screen" or "Install."
